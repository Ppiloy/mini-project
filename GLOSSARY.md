# Foreign Tax Credit prototype

Terms used in the supplied project slides and the prototype plan. Tax treatment depends on the applicable rule and tax year.

## People and records

**Taxpayer (ผู้เสียภาษี)**:
The individual whose foreign income and tax information are represented in an FTC case.

**Reviewing officer (เจ้าหน้าที่ผู้สอบทาน)**:
The role that inspects a taxpayer's submitted information and calculation result. Reviewing information does not itself establish an approved tax assessment.

**FTC case (คำขอคำนวณเครดิตภาษี)**:
The taxpayer's collection of income, tax, supporting evidence, and calculation information for a specified tax year.
_Avoid_: Tax return, when referring only to the prototype's calculation case.

## Income and credit

**Foreign income (เงินได้จากต่างประเทศ)**:
Income arising outside Thailand; it is distinct from the portion subsequently brought into Thailand.

**Remittance (เงินได้ที่นำเข้าประเทศไทย)**:
A transfer of money into Thailand associated with an identified foreign-income source. A remittance date and an income-earned date are different facts.

**Foreign tax paid (ภาษีที่ชำระในต่างประเทศ)**:
Tax actually paid abroad on the relevant foreign income. This is not automatically the amount creditable in Thailand.

**Foreign tax attributable to a remittance**:
The portion of relevant foreign tax allocated to the foreign income brought into Thailand under the applicable calculation rules.

**Credit limitation (เพดานเครดิตภาษี)**:
The applicable upper limit on foreign tax credit under the relevant rules.

**Allowable foreign tax credit (เครดิตภาษีที่ใช้ได้)**:
The amount of foreign tax that may offset Thai tax after applicable eligibility and limitation rules have been applied.
_Avoid_: Foreign tax paid, refund.

**Double taxation agreement (อนุสัญญาภาษีซ้อน; DTA)**:
A bilateral agreement allocating taxing rights and providing mechanisms to relieve double taxation, subject to its conditions.
_Avoid_: Country credit percentage.

**Tax year (ปีภาษี)**:
The annual period to which the case relates. Its Buddhist Era display and Gregorian representation identify the same year.

**Exchange-rate reference (อัตราแลกเปลี่ยนอ้างอิง)**:
The rate, quotation basis, applicable date, and source used to express an amount in another currency.
